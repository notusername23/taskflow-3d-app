Summary

TaskFlow 3D keeps your tasks and projects on your own device. It has no accounts, no cloud sync and no analytics, and we do not receive, sell or share your task data. The only sync is the optional Google Calendar connection, which sends tasks from your browser straight to your own Google account.

  • Stored locally: everything you enter lives in your browser's storage on the device you use.
  • No tracking: the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Outside services: the page loads fonts from Google Fonts, and syncs with Google Calendar only if you connect it (see Third-party services).
  • You are in control: you can edit, delete, reset or fully wipe your data at any time.

This policy applies to the current version of TaskFlow 3D, a single-user to-do application. Effective date: October 15, 2026.

Information the app handles

TaskFlow 3D only handles what you type into it:

  • Tasks: title, description, due date and time, priority, category, reminder choice, starred and completed state, and created, updated and completed timestamps.
  • Projects: name, description and colour.
  • Preferences: your display name, week start day, motion setting, 3D effects setting and sidebar state.

The app does not ask for your email address, phone number, location, contacts or any government or payment details. Please do not store passwords, card numbers or other highly sensitive details in task text, because the data is not encrypted (see Security).

Where your data is stored

Your data is saved only in your browser, on the device and browser profile you use. Tasks and projects go in IndexedDB; small preferences go in localStorage. Nothing is uploaded to a server we run, because there is no server.

This has practical consequences:

  • Clearing site data, using a private window, or uninstalling the browser can permanently delete your tasks.
  • Your data does not appear on other devices or in other browsers. TaskFlow has no cloud sync of its own; only tasks you push to Google Calendar are also stored by Google.
  • We cannot recover lost data, because we never hold a copy.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Fonts, which supplies the IBM Plex typefaces. When the page loads, your browser requests the fonts from Google's servers, so Google receives your IP address and standard browser request details. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy. If you self-host the fonts, this request goes away.

The app has no analytics, advertising networks, social plug-ins, crash reporters or other trackers, and it sends your tasks to no third party except Google Calendar, and only if you connect it.

Google Calendar integration

Connecting Google Calendar is optional and stays off until you start it in Settings.

  • Sign-in: you approve access on Google's own consent screen. TaskFlow never sees your Google password.
  • Access requested: permission to create, update and delete events in your calendar.
  • What is sent: for tasks you sync, the title, description, due date and time, and reminder go from your browser directly to Google. They do not pass through any TaskFlow server.
  • Sign-in token: the access token stays in your browser's memory for the session and is not saved to storage.
  • How Google data is used: only to provide the sync you asked for. It is not sold, used for advertising, used to train AI models, or shared with anyone else.
  • Disconnecting: use Disconnect in Settings, or remove TaskFlow at Google account permissions. Events already in your calendar stay there until you delete them.

TaskFlow's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Cookies, permissions and reminders

The app sets no cookies. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Reminders are shown inside the app only, as in-app notices when a reminder time has passed. The app does not send push notifications, emails or text messages.

Your controls

Because the data is yours and local, you manage it directly:

  • Edit or delete: change or remove any task or project in the app. Deleting a project asks whether to keep or delete its tasks.
  • Reset demo data: Settings can restore the sample tasks, replacing your current data.
  • Remove everything: Settings can clear all tasks and projects.
  • Browser level: clearing the site's data in your browser removes all stored data, including preferences.

Security

Data stays in your browser's storage and is protected by your device and browser, not by TaskFlow 3D. It is not encrypted by the app, and the app has no password or lock screen. Anyone who can use your browser profile, or any browser extension you allow to read this site, may be able to read your tasks. Use a device lock and keep your browser updated.

Children's privacy

TaskFlow 3D collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing leaves the device unless you connect Google Calendar, there is no child data for us to collect, use or delete. Parents and guardians remain responsible for what a child enters into the app.

Future features and changes

TaskFlow accounts and a TaskFlow cloud service are not part of this version; Google Calendar is the only sync. If they are added, your data would then be sent to and stored on a server, so this policy must be updated before that release, and the change should be clearly announced in the app.

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
